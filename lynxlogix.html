Version A — Signal Concierge

Who it serves

Owner who already knows the logic

Bots ingest alerts, normalize them, and write a one-screen brief: pair, venue, size, invalidation, fees, and why the signal can be ignored. You tap Approve / Skip / Reduce. Execution hits Coinbase Advanced or Kraken Spot only after the tap.

Bots

Scout watches webhooks. Scribe logs every alert. Risk caps notional. Counsel argues the opposite case. Clerk places the approved order. No clerk without a signed gate ticket.

Version B — Paper First, Live Later

Who it serves

Proof before pride

Same orchestra, but Clerk is locked to paper for N sessions. A daily report compares paper vs. what live would have done after fees and slippage. You promote a strategy by name, not by vibe. Kill switch is a single env flag.

Rule

If paper expectancy is not positive after fees for 20 sessions, the strategy stays a draft. “10x” is a backtest label you are not allowed to put on the homepage.

Version C — Multi-Venue Allocator

Who it serves

UHNW sleeve with envelopes

Capital is split into named envelopes: Core BTC/ETH, Satellite alts, Stable reserve, Experiment. Each envelope has max daily loss, max position, and allowed venues. Signals may only spend their envelope. Phantom is a treasury display, not a hot wallet for bots.

Venues

Coinbase Advanced Trade API and Kraken Spot/Futures (if enabled) for execution. Phantom + Kraken Wallet: deposit address watchers and manual sweep checklists. Never paste a seed. Never store a private key in Vercel env.

Human gate — non-negotiable

Required

Every live order needs: (1) signal id, (2) risk envelope check, (3) opposite-case note from Counsel, (4) your Approve with size, (5) Clerk receipt, (6) Scribe archive. If any step is missing, the desk idles. Automation is the queue. Authority is you.

What we will not build today

A fully autonomous live trader that hits market on every webhook. That is how accounts die and how regulators get interested. Also not: pooling other people’s money, promising 10x, or asking anyone for a seed phrase.
